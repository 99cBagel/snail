# Implement Plan —  KB template

## Top 5 questions my KB can address

### 1. Have you solved a problem like ours?

- What the prospect is really asking: is our situation familiar to you, or would we be your first?
- Answered by: `cases` + `credentials`
- Why my KB is good at it: situation-matched, anonymized proof — what the situation was, what was done, what resulted. Not claims, cases.
- Must not: name clients, disclose proprietary detail, or promise the same result.

### 2. How would you approach it? What do the first 30 / 90 days look like?

- What the prospect is really asking: do you have a method, or do you improvise?
- Answered by: `playbooks`
- Why my KB is good at it: a named, sequenced method — what happens first, second, third — instead of generic advice.
- Must not: present a method as a guarantee, or quote a timeline the consultant has not approved.

### 3. What goes wrong here? What should we watch for?

- What the prospect is really asking: can you see around corners we cannot?
- Answered by: `judgment`
- Why my KB is good at it: red flags, traps, and "when X, do Y because Z" rules from pattern recognition — the content generic AI cannot supply.
- Must not: diagnose the prospect's specific situation. My KB states general rules; applying them takes the consultant.

### 4. How do you handle an audit finding / CAPA / training gap / tech transfer?

- What the prospect is really asking: can you operate inside our regulated reality?
- Answered by: `playbooks` + `cases` + `judgment`
- Why my KB is good at it: regulated-process answers, owner-approved — exactly where generic AI is dangerous and specific experience matters.
- Must not: claim compliance outcomes, certifications, or regulatory approval on the prospect's behalf.

### 5. Are you the right fit — and what don't you do?

- What the prospect is really asking: should we talk at all, and can we trust your no?
- Answered by: `credentials` + `boundaries`
- Why my KB is good at it: honest scope builds trust and filters bad fits early — saving both sides a wasted meeting.
- Must not: stretch scope to win the inquiry. A clean no is a feature.

## My KB structure — 5 docs

| Doc | Holds | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `credentials` | Roles, domains, certifications — confirmed facts only | Interview Part 1 |
| `cases` | Situation → action → result, anonymized | Interview Part 2 |
| `playbooks` | Repeatable methods, sequenced, with traps | Interview Part 3 |
| `judgment` | "When X, do Y because Z" rules, in the principal's voice | Interview Part 4 |
| `boundaries` | What the consultancy does not do, will not claim | Interview Part 5 |

Optional sixth doc: `engagement` — how engagements work, what a prospect should expect, contact route. Add only when the commercial terms are settled.

## KB implementation: Workflow

### Interviews — Parts 1 to 5 (TO BE FILLED IN)

Content comes from structured interviews with the principal. Each part maps to one doc in my KB. Status of every part below: **pending — to be filled in**.

- ☐ **Part 1 — Credentials & scope.** Career timeline, roles, domains, certifications. What may be named, what must stay out. Who my KB serves.
- ☐ **Part 2 — Signature cases.** Three cases minimum. Same five prompts each: situation, actions, result, what made it hard, what may be shared and how anonymized.
- ☐ **Part 3 — Playbooks.** Repeatable methods: sequence, what gets looked at first, the traps. Assessment, training rollout, remediation, investigation, transfer.
- ☐ **Part 4 — Judgment rules.** Red flags, common mistakes, decision rules with reasoning. Capture exact wording.
- ☐ **Part 5 — Boundaries.** Turn-downs, claims never made, topics outside scope, review process for KB drafts.

Rules for every interview:

- Permission to record / take verbatim notes, agreed before starting.
- Nothing enters my KB without the principal's review and approval.
- Client names and proprietary detail stay out unless explicitly cleared. Default: anonymize.
- After each session: transcribe the same day, mark every passage with its source question.

## Grounding rules (my KB's contract with every reader)

- Answer only from my KB passages. Quote prices, terms, and credentials exactly as written.
- If my KB does not cover a question, say so plainly. Never fill the gap with general knowledge presented as the consultancy's position.
- Never invent a benefit, a case, or an outcome. Never promise results.
- Never claim regulatory approval, certification, or compliance on anyone's behalf.
- The principal's quality system — and the prospect's — stays in charge. My KB advises; humans decide.
