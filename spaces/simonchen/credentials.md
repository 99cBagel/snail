## Roles

- **Presenter International Inc.** [eDetailing APP and Salesforce Effectiveness Tools], 1999–2013 — Consultant. Hired to develop the new product launch plan. Designed the content plan for the new mobile SFE for pharmaceutical sales representatives. Completed assigned projects and tasks using standardized processes within timeline and budget. Conducted formal and informal presentations to clients. Built working relationships with mega brand key accounts, e.g. Lipitor, Aricept, and Xeloda.
- **Philogensis Inc.** [bio-based medical device, CABG coronary artery bypass graft], 1999–2013 — Consultant. Initially hired to work on investment financing; later also assumed responsibility for cost control and transition management. Participated in the ISO certification. Facilitated the 510K (class III medical device) application to FDA.
- **BioMax Inc.** [collagen hemostat], 1999–2013 — Member of the board of directors. Implemented financial management with success; the company was later acquired by a Taiwanese public-listed consumer product company.
- **The Yellow Wristband Campaign** [National Awareness for Alzheimer's and other Dementia], 2012–2024 — Consultant and Project Manager. Hired to design a strategic plan and lead the Project team to develop a novice wearable GPS/QR tracker ("Yellow Wristband"). Guided the team from initial concepts through transition to full-scale production. Established systems for monitoring and reporting on production costs. Oversaw the procurement process and enforced budgeting control over the BOM acquisition process and throughout the engineering efforts. Based in Beijing until COVID in 2020, then managed the project remotely from the US until it ended in 2024.
- Selected results: quoted as a guidance example in *Dementia Friendly Communities* (2017, global edition) — https://www.alzint.org/u/dfc-developments.pdf
- To this date, over 500,000 bands have been given out free. Every year, thousands of wandering-lost would get help, and millions of hits would fall on the web site.
- **3G Cellular Inc.**, San Diego, CA [RF board-level engineering house], 1995–1998 — Controller and CFO. In charge of overall cost control. Kept the organization financially stable and sound, including effective management of purchase and engineering resources. Maintained investor relationships.
- **Nesmith Enterprise (PBS Home Video)**, Los Angeles, CA [PBS franchise], 1990–1994 — Controller. Responsible for accounting reports and ad hoc analysis. Maintained cost/budget by product. Prepared quarterly royalty and residuals reports to the licensors, the creation guilds, and other parties.
- **Empire International**, Hollywood, CA [theatrical films and distribution], 1988–1989 — Internal Auditor.
