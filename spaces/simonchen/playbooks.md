# Playbooks

Status: Assessment approved and published 2026-11-17 (Interview Part 3). The remaining playbooks are still to fill. Each method: the sequence, what gets looked at first, the traps.

## Assessment

- **Sequence:** Catch their pain points first. Understand the budget limit. Survey their understanding of technology. Then show my pitch — with the words like "novice technology", "new tools".
- **Look at first:** Most of my clients are marketing background in pharma. They know of my past experience, and will usually listen for novice and innovation. Marketing prepares their budget by year, and every year they like to stage something different from the previous year — ideas and novelty attract their attention.
- **Traps:** Watch out for low-budget customers. Watch out for people making a move to another position — they usually do not have passion for a large project.
